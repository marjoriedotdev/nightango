Torch Client

About the Client

The Torch Client is a free, non-profit, open-source JavaScript injected web-client for Chatango.

It aims to enhance the experience by improving security, adding new features and fixing bugs that Chatango has not.

Note that some aspects are intangible, so I am very much limited in what I can improve, add or remove.

Development initially began on the 26th of April, 2021.

Despite some of my previous work having been published, this is the first project I have made that was intended for public use, so I hope to uphold its integrity and quality by having the community in mind.

This project can branch out to make the client more accessible with its own app or downloadable client if it is highly requested and necessary.

I want users to have a valuable experience investing their time and money into Chatango.

My 8 years on the site have shown me that people simply deserve more, so I've used my self-taught abilities to give them what they deserve.
